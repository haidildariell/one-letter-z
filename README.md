# One Letter for Your Day — Z

Z stands for "zero sleep" — and for "zombie". One sharp picture of a day with no sleep at all: eyes half open, brain offline, shambling through the hours. Funny, vivid, and true.

## Why Z

Z beats vaguer letters like A for "amazing" because A could mean anything — a good day, a great meal, a nice walk. Z points at one sharp picture: zero sleep, zombie mode, a day lived on fumes. The judge rewards vivid, funny, true — and a letter that paints a single unmistakable scene wins over a letter that means everything and nothing.
